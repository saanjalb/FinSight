## What it is
An emergency fund is money set aside only for unexpected events such as job loss, a medical bill, or urgent repairs. It is not meant for planned purchases or investing.

## How much to keep
A common guideline is three to six months of essential expenses (rent, groceries, bills, EMIs, insurance). If your essentials are ₹30,000 a month, the target range is ₹90,000 to ₹1,80,000. People with irregular income or dependants often aim for the higher end.

## Where to keep it
The money should be safe and easy to reach quickly, for example a savings account or a fixed deposit that can be broken early. Growth is less important than availability, so it is usually not kept in volatile investments.

## Building it
Start small and add a fixed amount every month. Treat the contribution like a bill so it happens automatically.
