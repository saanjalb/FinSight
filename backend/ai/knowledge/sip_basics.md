## What it is
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a way of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, usually monthly, into a mutual fund. It is a method of investing, not a separate product.

## How it works
On a set date each month, the chosen amount is automatically moved from your bank account into the fund, and you receive units at that day's price. For example, ₹5,000 a month buys more units when prices are low and fewer when prices are high.

## Rupee cost averaging
Because the amount is fixed, the average cost per unit over time is smoothed out. This reduces the need to time the market, but it does not remove risk or guarantee a profit.

## Things to remember
Mutual fund returns are linked to markets and are not guaranteed. The value can fall as well as rise, and SIPs are generally meant for longer time horizons. This is general education, not advice about which fund to choose.
