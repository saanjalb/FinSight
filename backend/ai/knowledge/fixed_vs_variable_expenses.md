---
title: Fixed vs Variable Expenses
source: FinSight educational summary (written for this project)
---
## Fixed expenses
Fixed expenses stay about the same every month and are hard to change in the short term. Examples are rent, loan EMIs, insurance premiums, and subscriptions billed at a set amount.

## Variable expenses
Variable expenses change from month to month and are easier to adjust. Examples are food delivery, shopping, fuel, travel, and entertainment.

## Semi-variable expenses
Some costs have a fixed part and a variable part, such as electricity or mobile bills that depend on usage.

## Why the difference matters
When you need to cut spending quickly, variable expenses are the practical place to start because fixed expenses are committed. A high share of fixed expenses leaves less room to adjust if income drops, so it helps to track how much of your income is already committed before the month begins.
